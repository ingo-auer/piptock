Piptock privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Piptock is an egg timer app made by Ingo Auer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Piptock does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and nothing you enter in the app is sent to me or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your settings and choices, such as egg size, start temperature, altitude, alarm sound and recent timers, are stored on your iPhone only.

Notifications and Live Activities

Piptock uses notifications and Live Activities to tell you when your egg is ready. These are scheduled on your device and do not involve a server.

Third parties

Piptock does not include advertising, analytics or tracking software, and does not share data with third parties.

Children

Piptock does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: piptock@auer.co.uk.
