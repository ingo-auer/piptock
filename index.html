Common questions

Why does my egg time differ from another timer?
Piptock adjusts for egg size, whether the egg starts in the fridge or at room temperature, and your altitude. Change these in the setup screens to match your kitchen.

The countdown isn't showing on my Lock Screen.
Check that Live Activities are allowed for Piptock in Settings > Piptock, and that notifications are on.
